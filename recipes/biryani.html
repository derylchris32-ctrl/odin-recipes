Biryani Recipe

Here is a simple recipe for making delicious biryani!

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water until the water runs clear. Soak the rice in water for 30 minutes.
  2. In a large pan, heat vegetable oil over medium heat. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add minced garlic and ginger-garlic paste to the pan. Stir well and let it cook for a minute.
  4. Add crushed tomatoes and garam masala to the pan. Stir well and let it simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  5. Drain the soaked rice and add it to the pan. Stir well to combine all ingredients.
  6. Season the biryani with salt to taste.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy your homemade biryani!
